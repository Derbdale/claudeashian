# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claude Code v2.1.215** — Released July 19, 2026. Claude no longer runs the `/verify` and `/code-review` skills automatically; invoke them explicitly with `/verify` or `/code-review` when you want them. (Source: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)
- **Fable 5 subscription model finalized: Max permanent, Pro credits-only** — After the July 19, 2026 promotional window closed, Anthropic settled Fable 5 into a permanent tiered structure. Max plans, premium Team seats, and premium Enterprise seats include Fable 5 as a standard part of the plan (up to 50% of weekly usage limits). Pro plans, standard Team seats, and standard Enterprise seats must use pay-as-you-go usage credits at standard API rates ($10/$50 per MTok). Pro and standard Team users receive a one-time promotional credit offset. (Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15424964-claude-fable-5-on-your-plan)
