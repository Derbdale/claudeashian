# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Anthropic partners with Accenture on embedded AI evaluation** — Announced September 18, 2026. Accenture becomes Anthropic's first external "embedded evaluator," with employee-level access inside Anthropic to watch models take shape in training, observe deployment decisions, red-team models, conduct alignment assessments, test safeguards, and report incidents to the public. Both companies each commit at least $1 billion over five years to build evaluation capacity. The arrangement makes Anthropic's safety commitments "more verifiable" without transferring accountability. Non-exclusive: Anthropic is in dialogue with METR and other nonprofits, and will announce additional embedded evaluators. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation)
