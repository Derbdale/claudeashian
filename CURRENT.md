# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claude Code v2.1.227** — Released August 10, 2026. Key fixes and improvements: feature flags no longer evaluated without the user's subscription tier when a session starts with an expired login token; Bash commands no longer fail under `claude-code-action` with `allowed_non_write_users` on GitHub-hosted runners; `/tui` no longer resurfaces conversations rewound before their first message; slash-command menu improved (blue highlights only the selected row, matched characters bolded, emoji/accented names render correctly); fewer event-loop stalls on file-not-found suggestions and at-mention size checks. (Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)
- **Auto mode becomes default for all Pro/Max/Team accounts on August 14** — Anthropic announced that auto mode will be enabled by default for interactive Pro, Max, and Team Claude Code sessions starting August 14, 2026. Previously, manual approval at each step was the default. In auto mode, Claude proceeds without prompts unless an action is irreversible, destructive, or targets outside your environment. Internal testing with 1,053 paid testers found automated safety measures caught 89% of harmful actions vs. 13.6% for human review, and that users approved 97% of prompts anyway. New safeguards being added alongside: prompt injection screening and customisable hard deny rules. (Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/)
- **$9.1B Riot Platforms data center deal** — Announced August 10–11, 2026. Anthropic struck a 20-year deal with Riot Platforms Inc. to lease 191 megawatts of IT capacity at Riot's Rockdale, Texas campus through June 2048, valued at $9.1 billion over the initial term. Two optional five-year extensions could raise the total to $16.1 billion. Delivery: first 96 MW targeted for December 2027, full 191 MW by June 2028. Riot secured a $573 million Morgan Stanley interim financing facility to fund development. The deal is Riot's second major data center contract in 2026, following an earlier AMD agreement. (Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/anthropic-strikes-9-billion-deal-with-cloud-computing-firm-riot)
