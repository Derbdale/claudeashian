- **Anthropic revises enterprise data retention policy after pushback** — Announced August 20–21, 2026. Since June, Anthropic had stored all data from its flagship Mythos and Fable model requests on its own servers for 30 days to monitor for cyberattacks — a policy it admitted was "unpopular and a business risk." Under the revised policy, enterprise customers can instead keep the 30-day safety-monitoring data within their own cloud infrastructure rather than on Anthropic's servers. Developed in coordination with over 100 enterprise customers including Salesforce. A new safety monitoring system enabling this is expected to roll out in fall 2026. (Source: https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-changes-data-retention-policy-after-enterprise-pushback/)
