# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claudeforce: Salesforce and Anthropic expanded partnership** — Announced August 26-27, 2026. The two companies deepened their strategic partnership, branding the integration "Claudeforce." Claude moves into Salesforce as a reasoning model; Salesforce moves into Claude as a plugin with 37 prebuilt sales skills (meeting prep, deal health review, pipeline review) using MCP for real-time data access. Salesforce plans to spend approximately $300 million on Anthropic tokens in 2026 (in addition to its existing $300 million equity stake). Claude is Salesforce's preferred AI for developers and knowledge workers; Anthropic has adopted Salesforce as its preferred CRM. Open beta for Salesforce in Claude expected September 2026. (Source: https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/salesforce-and-anthropic-announce-claudeforce/)
- **Claude for Teachers expanded to schools and districts** — August 28, 2026. Anthropic extended its July 14 Claude for Teachers program (for individual educators) into a free Enterprise offering for K-12 schools and districts. Schools get centralised single sign-on, role-based access controls, and standards-aligned teaching tools. Two new teaching skills added: lesson preparation and checks for understanding. FERPA-aligned data privacy protections included. Qualifying organizations signing up by June 30, 2027 receive a full year of free access. Piloting with Detroit Public Schools Community District this fall. (Source: https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-teachers-now-available-for-schools-and-districts)
