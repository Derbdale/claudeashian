# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claude for Manufacturing launched** — Plugin for Team and Enterprise plans with 17 AI skills spanning sales, quality, operations, supply chain, design, and setup. Connectors include Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, and Oracle NetSuite. All outputs require human review before reaching customers or suppliers; Claude does not control equipment directly. Technical webinar November 17, 2026. (Source: https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-for-manufacturing)
- **Claude Personal Finance launched (beta)** — Read-only beta for US Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers connecting bank and card accounts via Plaid. Claude sees account names, last 4 digits, balances, and up to 2 years of transaction history. Conversations not used for model training; no ads, no investment products pushed. Not investment advice. Launched November 5, 2026; earlier testing name was "Claude Money". (Source: https://betanews.com/article/claude-personal-finance-bank-accounts-plaid/)
