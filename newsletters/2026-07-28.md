# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Anthropic publishes position on open-weights models** — Published July 27, 2026 amid an industry debate after 50+ companies including Nvidia, Meta, and Microsoft signed "Open Weights and American AI Leadership." Anthropic stated it has "never advocated for a ban on open-weights models" and instead supports three targeted policies: chip export restrictions to limit adversarial nations from training frontier models, crackdowns on industrial-scale distillation attacks, and mandatory safety testing for all sufficiently capable models (open or closed) before release. Dario Amodei argued the core risk is authoritarian governments building superior AI, not open-weights releases themselves. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)
- **Cognizant-Anthropic Global Premier Partnership expansion** — Announced July 27, 2026; Cognizant achieved Global Premier Partner status in the Claude Partner Network and is embedding Claude across its Flowsource, Neuro AI Engineering, and Neuro IT Ops platforms. Over 30,000 associates have completed Claude training, with deployment spanning manufacturing, life sciences, insurance, and biopharmaceutical verticals — one insurance use case saves underwriters approximately eight hours per week. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-anthropic)
