# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claude Code v2.1.248** — Released August 28, 2026. New "restricted mode" removes built-in command and code tools for high-security or minimal-surface deployments; cross-session messaging improvements; a usage credits command for Enterprise to query spend from the CLI. Key prompt caching fix: OAuth token refreshes were causing one cache miss per hour in long sessions, now resolved. Also fixes Desktop sessions disappearing from the agents view after 30 days and intermittent Remote Control message delivery failures. (Source: https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)
- **Model Hardware Standard (MHS) research preview** — Announced August 27, 2026. Anthropic launched a research preview of MHS, a shared specification enabling AI agents to safely operate physical lab and manufacturing equipment (microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, quantum laser systems) in parallel. Reduces hardware integration time from weeks or months to hours. Works via MCP, command line, and code APIs; model-agnostic. Development began with HHMI Janelia Research Campus; early partners include Genentech, Carnegie Mellon, University of Washington, QuEra Computing, and Tetsuwan Scientific. Available to select labs via waitlist; Anthropic plans to open-source MHS once safety evaluations and best practices are established. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
- **10,000 free and discounted Claude Team seats for scientists** — Announced August 27, 2026. Anthropic opened 10,000 Claude Team plan seats for researchers at academic and nonprofit institutions: standard seats are free; premium seats (5× usage limits) cost $15/month, fixed for one year. Eligible applicants are principal investigators who can add their lab members to the plan, covering natural sciences, mathematics, computer science, and engineering. Anthropic plans to expand beyond 10,000 seats in coming months. The AI for Science credit program (previously biology-focused) was simultaneously expanded to other compute-intensive research domains. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists)
