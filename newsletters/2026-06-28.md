# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.
- **Hook matchers with hyphenated identifiers now exact-match** — In v2.1.195 (June 26, 2026), hook tool matchers containing hyphens (e.g., `code-reviewer`, `mcp__brave-search`) changed from substring-matching to exact-matching. If you rely on a hyphenated MCP server prefix matching all its tools, update your hook config to use a wildcard: `mcp__brave-search__.*`. (Source: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)
- **Mythos 5 partially restored for US critical infrastructure** — On June 26, 2026, the US government authorized Anthropic to redeploy Claude Mythos 5 to over 100 US organisations (government agencies and private companies) operating and defending critical infrastructure. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick determined "appropriate safeguards are in place" for these trusted partners, reversing the June 12 ban for this sector. The authorisation extends to non-American employees at approved organisations. Claude Fable 5 remains fully suspended pending further negotiations; Anthropic stated it is "continuing to work with the government to expand access to Mythos 5 and make Fable 5 available for general use again." (Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/trump-admin-releases-anthropic-mythos-to-be-used-by-more-than-100-us-companies-agencies/)
- **Anthropic Economic Index "Cadences" report** — June 2026 report covering usage patterns across Claude's user base, based on ~9,700 linked survey respondents from mid-May to early June. Key findings: 93% of Claude conversations produce concrete outputs (explanations 17%, documents/reports 15%, guidance 11%); personal conversations rise from 35% on weekdays to nearly 50% on weekends; higher-wage occupations generate ~2.5× more tokens per conversation than lower-wage ones; Claude Code sessions show much higher AI autonomy than chat (users delegate whole tasks vs. iterative 13-round exchanges); users who delegate most heavily are the most optimistic about their career outcomes. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report)
