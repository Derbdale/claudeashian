# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Salesforce Hosted MCP Servers** — Generally available from April 2026, giving Claude structured access to Salesforce Platform, Data 360, Tableau, and MuleSoft via pre-built standard servers, plus custom server support through Apex Actions, Lightning Flows, REST endpoints, and templated prompts. Enables SOQL queries and record modification without accessing Salesforce's UI. (Source: https://developer.salesforce.com/blogs/2026/05/connect-claude-with-salesforce-hosted-mcp-servers)
- **Anthropic First Public Record** — Anthropic published its inaugural "Public Record" on June 12, 2026 — an evolving transparency initiative sharing internal documentation on AI safety, technical specifications, and alignment research. Accompanying survey: 48% of Americans hope AI cures cancer, but only 15% trust AI companies to make key decisions; 64% fear job loss and 70%+ demand government regulation; daily work AI users are 16 points less concerned about dependency than non-users. Future installments will cover new research findings and development updates. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-public-record)
- **"Policy on the AI Exponential" by Dario Amodei** — Two-part policy paper published June 10, 2026. The Advanced AI Framework proposes FAA-style mandatory third-party safety testing and government authority to block dangerous frontier model releases, covering models trained on more than 10^25 FLOPs by companies earning over $500M in AI revenue or spending over $1B on research. Four mandatory risk categories: biological weapons, cybersecurity, loss of control, and automated AI R&D. The Economic Policy Framework addresses labor-market transition; Anthropic pledged $350M: a $200M Economic Futures Research Fund and a $150M national fellowship program. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/policy-on-the-ai-exponential)
