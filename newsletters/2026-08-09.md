# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Managed Agents per-agent `inference_geo` setting** — Claude Managed Agents agents can now set `inference_geo` inside the `model` object when creating an agent, or override it for a single session, bringing data residency controls to the per-agent level in the Managed Agents API. Complements the existing Messages API `inference_geo` parameter (August 7, 2026). (Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/overview)
- **Anthropic-Google-Broadcom compute partnership** — Announced April 6, 2026; Anthropic's largest compute commitment to date, securing multiple gigawatts of next-generation Google TPU capacity expected to launch in 2027. At announcement, run-rate revenue exceeded $30B (up from ~$9B at end-2025) and enterprise customers spending $1M+ annually had doubled to over 1,000 in less than two months. Extends Anthropic's November 2025 pledge to invest $50B in US AI infrastructure; most new capacity will be in the US. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute)
- **SpaceX Colossus 1 compute deal and higher Claude usage limits** — Announced May 6, 2026; Anthropic secured access to SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center, adding 300+ MW of new capacity (over 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs) within one month. Simultaneously, Claude Code's five-hour rate limits doubled for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans, and peak-hour rate reductions were eliminated for Pro and Max users. Part of a broader set of compute agreements with Amazon, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and SpaceX. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/higher-limits-spacex)
