# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Anthropic doubles Public First Action funding to $40M total** — On July 21, 2026, Anthropic announced a second $20 million donation to Public First Action, an organisation that advocates for AI risk mitigation, bringing its total contribution to $40 million (the first $20M was donated in February 2026). The funds support public education and policy work on AI and cannot be used to influence candidate elections. The announcement came ahead of US midterm elections as companies compete over the scope of AI regulation. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/donate-public-first-action)
- **AI for Science rare disease research grants** — Announced July 20, 2026; Anthropic's AI for Science program is offering up to $50,000 in Claude credits over six months to researchers working on rare genetic diseases. Applications close August 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST. Two tracks: Basic Science (disease mechanism discovery, outputs shared with Monarch Initiative) and Biotech (accelerating drug development and regulatory documentation). Early adopters include Every Cure (drug repurposing), Centre for Population Genomics (variant classification), and Violet Research Institute (FDA guidance analysis). (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/rare-disease-research-grants)
- **Economic Futures Research Fund publishes research agenda** — On July 22, 2026, Anthropic published the specific research agenda for its $200M Economic Futures Research Fund (announced June 10). Five priority areas: shaping AI's impact on workers at the firm level; equipping people to navigate AI-driven transitions; modernising income support for displacement; building worker stakes in AI-driven growth before disruption arrives; and generating new evidence on public investments. Grants will primarily be in the $5–30 million range for large-scale randomised controlled trials; eligible applicants include universities, research institutes, policy organisations, and non-profits with field experiment track records. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/economic-futures-research-fund-agenda)
