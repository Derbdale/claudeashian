# Current Discoveries

New items discovered since the last newsletter.

- **Claude Code weekly limits permanently raised to 125% of baseline from September 14** — Announced August 29, 2026. The temporary 50% weekly limit boost (active since May 2026) ends September 14, replaced by a permanent 25% increase above the pre-promotion baseline. In practice this is a net ~17% reduction compared to today's boosted level: if the original baseline was 100 units, the promo gave 150 — the new permanent level is 125. Five-hour rolling limits are unaffected; free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats are excluded. (Source: https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2093742321473065266)
